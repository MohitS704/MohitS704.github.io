The Gritsan group focuses on precision studies of the Higgs boson and its interactions within
what is known as the "Golden Channel" of Higgs boson decays (due to the cleanliness
of the decay products), $$H \rightarrow ZZ \rightarrow 4\ell$$. 

My focus is on *off-shell* production of the Higgs boson, specifically
in the region above $$2m_Z \approx 180 GeV$$. This region is rich with new events
from Run-3 of the LHC, and is often used to measure the resonant decay 
width of the Higgs boson, which I will explain later.
