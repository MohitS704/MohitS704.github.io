I often find people who write blogs to be somewhat conceited and self-serving.
This is exactly why I am starting this site.

Unlike a Substack, a Github pages website allows me to also make this more of a personal
webpage in general, which just makes it more versatile. Similarly, I have zero intention of
adding any paywalls to this, since it just makes life harder.

I am starting this to do 2 things:
  1. Be conceited and self-serving
  2. Try and talk about my work?
  3. Serve as a personal website

We'll see where this takes us. I am sure that there is a relatively large likelihood this goes the way
a lot of blogs do, where it dies after a few posts. We shall see.
